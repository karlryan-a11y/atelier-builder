# SOP — Client ↔ Stylist Chat (for Stylists)
**Audience:** Lead and assistant stylists

This is how you message clients through Atelier. You never leave Slack. Clients message you from the Atelier app on their phone; their messages land in your Slack channel; you reply in the thread; they get it instantly. Everything is logged automatically.

## The one-minute version
1. A client message shows up as a **new thread** in your client channel, headed with the client's name.
2. **Reply in that thread.** Your reply goes straight to the client's phone.
3. Reply **within 2 business hours.** If you can't, someone will be nudged to cover.
4. Keep Slack notifications **on** on your phone — that's how you know a client messaged.

## Setup (do this once)
- [ ] Install **Slack on your phone** and turn **push notifications ON** for your client channel. Non-negotiable — it's how you find out a client messaged you. Treat a muted channel like a missed phone.
- [ ] Confirm you're a member of your **client channel** (e.g. `#sarah-clients`) and of **`#escalations`**.
- [ ] Set your **business hours and timezone** with Karl so the system knows when your response clock runs.

## How to respond to a client
1. You get a Slack push: a new message in your client channel.
2. Open the **thread** — it's headed with the client's name. The whole conversation with that client lives in this one thread.
3. **Reply in the thread.** What you type is what the client sees. Write it like you're texting the client directly — because you are.
4. The client gets it instantly (live if their app is open, as a notification + badge if it's closed).

**Always reply in the thread, never in the main channel.** A message in the main channel does **not** reach the client and breaks the conversation routing.

### Sending a look
You can drop a look card straight into the chat from the dashboard ("here's the dress I mentioned"). Clients can also send a look into the chat to say "more like this." Use it — it's the best part of the experience.

### Photos
Clients will send photos. You can send images back. They flow through like any message.

## Response time — the 2-hour rule
- **Target: reply within 2 business hours.** That's the promise the client sees in the app.
- The clock only runs **during your business hours.** A message at 11pm does not count against you overnight.
- Even a quick "Got this — I'll get you a full answer this afternoon 💛" counts as a response and resets the clock. **A fast acknowledgment beats a slow perfect answer.**
- The client already gets an automatic "Got it, {you} typically replies within a couple hours" the instant they hit send, so you have a little breathing room — but the real reply still needs to come inside the window.

## What happens if a message goes unanswered
1. **Stage 1 — you get a direct nudge.** A quiet re-ping to you specifically. Just respond.
2. **Stage 2 — it goes to `#escalations`.** If it's *still* unanswered, the whole team sees "⏰ {Client} has been waiting — tap to respond." Backup coverage, not a callout — but a client is hanging, so it matters.

### Covering for each other (`#escalations`)
- When you see an escalation, **anyone available claims it** by hitting the **"I've got it"** button (or 👀 react). That tells everyone else to stand down.
- If it's **your** client, you're still primary — but if you're heads-down or off, a teammate claiming it keeps the client cared for.
- `#escalations` shows **only the client's name and wait time — never the message content.** To read and reply, tap through to the conversation.

**Claim it or cover it — don't assume someone else will.**

## Onboarding a client to the app (do it in person)
The client's experience only works if the Atelier app is **added to their iPhone Home Screen** with **notifications allowed.** Don't leave this to a written instruction — **do it on their phone when you're with them**, or walk them through it live:
1. Open their Atelier login in **Safari** and have them log in once.
2. Tap the **Share** icon (square with the up arrow) → scroll to **"Add to Home Screen"** → **Add.**
3. Open the app **from the new Home Screen icon** (not the Safari tab).
4. When it asks to **allow notifications, tap Allow.** This gives them the red message badge and lock-screen alerts.
5. Send them a quick test message so they see it land.

Once that's done it behaves like a real app: tap the icon, message you, see their looks, get a red badge when you reply. Most clients will never deal with a login screen again.

> If a client says they "got logged out": they probably hadn't opened it in a couple weeks. One-tap fix — they request a magic link and they're back in. Reassure them.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- Keep Slack push on, always.
- Reply in the thread.
- Acknowledge fast, even if the full answer comes later.
- Claim escalations for teammates when you're free.
- Onboard clients to the Home Screen app in person.

**Don't**
- Don't reply in the main channel (the client won't get it).
- Don't paste client message content into `#escalations`.
- Don't let a channel sit muted on your phone.
- Don't promise a turnaround faster than 2 business hours unless you mean it.

## Questions / problems
Ping Karl in Slack. If something's broken (a client says they didn't get your reply, badges not showing, etc.), flag it — the system logs everything, so issues are traceable.
